# TraceNews Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 · Effective 4 October 2026**

This policy explains what personal information TraceNews collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. We have tried to write it in plain language. If something is unclear, ask us at info@tracenews.ng.

## 1. Who we are

TraceNews is operated by **TraceNews Limited**, a company registered in Nigeria. We are the "data controller" for the personal information described in this policy. Our registration details are available on request.

- Privacy questions and requests: **info@tracenews.ng**
- Data Protection Officer: **Toluwalope Bello, dpo@tracenews.ng**
- Regulator: the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)**

## 2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the TraceNews website at tracenews.ng and your TraceNews account. It has two parts: how we handle **your** information as a reader (sections 3 to 11), and how we handle information about **public figures and news outlets** that appears on our pages (section 12).

## 3. What we collect, why, and how long we keep it

| What | Examples | Why we use it | Our lawful basis | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Account details** | Your email address; a display name if you give one; your password (stored only in scrambled form, so nobody at TraceNews can read it); an internal account ID | To create and secure your account and let you sign in | Contract: we need it to give you the account you asked for | Until you delete your account. Backup copies are overwritten within 30 days of deletion |
| **Your settings and saved items** | Theme (light or dark); stories you save; topics you follow; alert preferences | To give you the features you chose | Contract | Until you remove them or delete your account |
| **Messages and correction requests** | The details you send us through the correction form or by email: your email address, your name if you give it, what you tell us | To read, answer and act on what you send | Our legitimate interest in handling accuracy and complaints properly, and our legal obligations | For as long as needed to deal with your request and any related complaint or legal claim, and in any case no longer than six years |
| **Technical and security records** | Your IP address, browser and device type, the time of a request | To keep the service secure, working and free from abuse | Our legitimate interest in security and reliability | No longer than 30 days |

We do **not** collect your precise location, your contacts, your payment details, or your microphone or camera. We do not run advertising on TraceNews and we do not build advertising profiles. If this changes we will tell you first and, where the law requires, ask for your consent.

## 4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only what is needed to run the site: a sign-in cookie or token that keeps you signed in, and your light or dark theme setting stored in your browser. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

## 5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. We use a small number of service providers to run TraceNews. Each processes information only to provide its service to us:

| Provider | What it does for us | Where it operates |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database and sign-in | Ireland |
| Railway | Application hosting | The Netherlands |
| Vercel | Website delivery | A network of servers in several countries, which may include countries outside Nigeria |
| Resend | Sends account emails and any alerts you ask for | The United States |

We also use an AI provider, **OpenAI**, which is based in the United States, to help produce the AI-generated summaries on our pages. We send it the text of published news articles. **We do not send it your account details.**

We may disclose information if a court, regulator or other authority lawfully requires it, to protect our legal rights, or to protect someone's safety. We assess every request for lawfulness and disclose only what we are legally required to.

## 6. Transfers outside Nigeria

Your information is stored and processed in **Ireland and the Netherlands**. Some of it also goes to providers in the **United States**: our email service, the AI provider described in section 5. We transfer personal information abroad only where the destination provides adequate protection for it, or where we have safeguards in place that give you equivalent protection, as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 requires.

## 7. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, access controls, and logging of access to the most sensitive data. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights and freedoms at risk, we will notify the NDPC and the people affected as the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct information that is wrong;
- delete your information;
- restrict how we use it;
- give you your information in a portable, machine-readable file;
- stop using it where we rely on our legitimate interests;
- stop relying on your consent, where we rely on it, at any time and as easily as you gave it.

We make no decisions about you by purely automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

To use a right, email **info@tracenews.ng**. We will reply within **30 days**. We may need to confirm that you are the account holder. It is free unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive. You can delete your account yourself inside the product.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first at info@tracenews.ng or dpo@tracenews.ng. You also have the right to complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission**.

## 9. Children

TraceNews accounts are for people aged **18 and over**. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

## 10. How long we keep information

The table in section 3 gives the period for each kind of information. When a period ends, we delete the information or make it permanently anonymous.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We will post any updated policy here with a new version number and date. If a change is significant we will tell signed-in readers by email or on the site before it takes effect. If we ever ask for your consent to something new, we will ask separately and clearly; we will not treat a change to this policy as your consent.

## 12. Public figures and news outlets

TraceNews publishes information about public figures, mainly politicians and office-holders, and about Nigerian news outlets. This information comes from published news coverage and public records. It describes how coverage has been distributed; it does not pass judgement on anyone. We handle this information separately from information about our readers, and we never link the two.

If you are named on a TraceNews page and believe something is factually wrong, such as an out-of-date office or party, a story attributed to the wrong person, or a wrong figure or date, use **Request a correction** at tracenews.ng/corrections or email **corrections@tracenews.ng**. We acknowledge requests within one business day and give a substantive response within five business days. You also have the rights in section 8 over any personal information we hold about you, and you can use the contacts below.
